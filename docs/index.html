The story to keep repeating

Kitty helps people who already trust each other save in a circle. Everyone contributes a set amount each round, and members take turns receiving the pot. Shared rules and a shared record make the commitment easier to follow.

People, not personas

You and your friends can use your own names, wherever you are from. No costumes, accents, flags or invented family history. In the scripts, Host, Friend A and Friend B are roles you can replace.

The circle is called Friday Circle. Its members save for their own goals; the pot rotates between members. It is not a shared travel budget or a bill-splitting app.

One clear starting point

Lead with a familiar question: who has paid, who receives this round, and what happens next? Show contributions, the payout order, and the record before advanced offers, sending, or technical architecture.

English is the current app language. “Somewhere else” in onboarding is a profile choice, not evidence of local payment support or approval to operate in that country.

A video without a shoot

The included 90-second animated concept uses the existing Kitty logo, bundled fonts, diagrams and sample values. Play it, scrub it, switch aspect ratio, or render it frame by frame into an MP4. No camera, microphone, location or paid video generator is needed for the silent first draft.

Two different jobs. A motion explainer communicates the idea. A working-product or sponsor demo needs real evidence of the claimed action. Use genuine screen captures and transaction records for that evidence; label mock screens and compressed time.

Friends can review the draft asynchronously or run a testnet step on their own phone. They never need to appear on camera or share a Google account. Optional native proof capture is described separately in the production guide.
