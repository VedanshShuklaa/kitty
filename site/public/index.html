kitty

A savings circle for people who already trust each other. Nobody holds the money.

Everyone puts in the same amount each round, and each round one person takes the whole pot, until everyone has had a turn. Sign in with your fingerprint; invites go out on WhatsApp.

Download for Android

How a circle works

  1. Start a circle

    Name it, add your people, pick the amount and how often.

  2. Send the invites

    Everyone gets their own WhatsApp link.

  3. Pay each round

    Everyone puts in, and one person takes the pot.

  4. Everyone gets a turn

    Deposits cover a missed payment and come back at the end.
