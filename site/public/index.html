kitty

A savings circle for people who already trust each other. Nobody holds the money.

Get Kitty for Android

How a circle works

  1. Start a circleName it, add the people you already save with, pick an amount and how often.
  2. Send the invitesEach person gets their own link on WhatsApp. It opens Kitty and takes their place.
  3. Everyone pays, one person takes the potEach round, the pot goes to the next person in line, or to whoever offers to give up a little to take it sooner.
  4. Everyone gets a turnDeposits cover a missed payment. At the end, deposits come back.

Kitty runs on Monad testnet with test dollars. No real money moves.
