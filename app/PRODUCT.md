# Kitty

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

People who already trust each other and organize rotating savings circles. The existing product brief prioritizes small displays, varied reading confidence, older Android phones and interrupted connections.

## Product Purpose

Help members understand their obligations, pay each round, follow who receives the pot and collect eligible balances when their circle finishes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing Expo / React Native app uses Mera passkeys, sponsored testnet gas, Agora test AUSD and Envio-backed history. Preserve authentication, approvals, financial actions, standing terms, amounts, retries and disclosures. Testnet balances have no cash value; yield is simulated. Native authentication and accessibility require separate device verification.

## Brand Commitments

Kitty's updated website (`site/public/index.html`) is the user's visual reference: white canvas, deep pink actions, soft pink tiles, warm room and pink cat, Bricolage Grotesque and Figtree. Local fonts and assets; no extra runtime UI dependencies. Preserve the current six-stage standing model and deterministic cat coats.

## Evidence on Hand

Current website and app source, local website reference capture, existing crypto/logic tests and isolated browser preview. Preview values and authentication are synthetic and must remain under `qa/`.

## Product Principles

- Financial clarity comes before decoration.
- Every screen identifies test dollars on Monad testnet.
- Unknown financial values stay unknown; success follows confirmation.
- The cat expresses standing without replacing payment language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep font scaling, wrapping text, visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, minimum 48dp touch targets and reduced-motion support. Browser layout checks do not certify native screen readers, passkeys or keyboard behavior.
